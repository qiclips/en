story synopsis1

“Soul Land” mainly depicts a fictional martial spirit world called Soul Land. There is no magic, no martial arts, no combat power, but there are magical martial spirits. Everyone here will perform martial spirit awakening when they are 6 years old, and since then these people who have awakened their martial spirits are collectively referred to as “spirit masters”. Martial spirits include animals, plants, and weapons. Martial spirits can assist people’s daily life, and people with special talents can use them to practice and fight. In this world, there is an evil organization that has been planning for many years and wants to dominate the world. Under the leadership of its leader Biyi Bidong, for the sake of power and desire, they established a “Spirit Empire”. They hunted and killed 100,000-year-old soul beasts in human form at will, and wantonly invaded the territory of other countries, causing the lives of the people to be destroyed and the people to suffer.

The righteous people led by the protagonist Tang San, the seven young people who roamed the continent in the name of the “Shrek Seven Devils”, constantly fought to protect their families and the country and protect the safety of the people. They constantly improved their strength and fought fiercely against the Spirit Empire ruled by Biyi Bidong. Tang San, as the best of the seven, started from the time his spirit awakened when he discovered that his martial spirit “Blue Silver Grass” was ridiculed as a useless martial spirit, and cultivated step by step, from man to god. Love and hatred were intertwined, blood and tears were bitter and bitter for years. In the end, Tang San led the entire “Shrek Seven Devils” regiment and the two major empires to eradicate the evil forces on the Soul Land, avenged their mother’s killing, and became the strongest on the Soul Land.

Tang Sect outer court disciple Tang San was forbidden by the Tang Sect because he secretly learned the inner court’s unique skills, but when he jumped off the cliff Mingzhi found that he was not dead, but instead came to another world with another identity, a world belonging to martial spirits, called Soul Land. There is no magic, no fighting spirit, no martial arts, but there are magical martial spirits. Everyone here, when they are six years old, will awaken their martial spirits in the Spirit Hall. Martial spirits have animals, plants, and weapons, and martial spirits can assist people’s daily life. And some of the particularly outstanding martial spirits can be used to cultivate and fight. This profession is the most powerful and most glorious profession “Spirit Master” on the Soul Land.

Little Tang San started his soul master cultivation path in the Holy Spirit Village, and had a dream of revitalizing the Tang Sect. When the Tang Sect hidden weapons came to Soul Land, when Tang San’s martial spirit awakened, could he cast the Tang Sect’s glory again in this martial spirit world…
